Zuck

The manager for Meta models. Bring a job, answer what Zuck asks until nothing is unknown, and Zuck plans it, staffs it with the right models, and runs it until it is done.

Sign in with handoff and your projects save to your account. Exit any time — come back, sign in, and pick up where you left off.

Got a job? Tell me what you want built and I will run it.

Zuck will ask about everything it does not know yet. Answer, skip what does not matter, or say plan it whenever you are ready.

Without a key, Zuck still plans everything and writes every brief — model tasks just wait with their brief ready. Zuck never stores your key, never logs it, and never puts it in a saved file. It is gone when you reload.

Zuck plans with Meta models: Spark for text at light, standard, or deep effort, Wonder for the hardest planning and review, Image for pictures, SAM 3.1 for vision, and you for the calls only a human makes. Gates hold: a task never starts until the work it depends on is done. Independent tasks run together.
